The William H. Endly Family

Harriet (Hattie) Endly

Daughter of William H. Endly and Sarah Ann Brooks

Harriet Endly was born 05 Apr 1839 in Guernsey County, Ohio.  She married William A. Lawrence 14 Mar 1860 in Guernsey County. They had these children:   Harry J. Lawrence, Frank A. Lawrence, William Brooks Lawrence, and Lottie Blanche Lawrence.

In the  1860 Census, Hattie is still living with her father and step mother in Guernsey County.  Also listed in that Census are M. Bellman, the mother of William H. Endly’s 3rd wife, Catherine Pedan, M. Brock, a five year old female that I cannot identify and J. H. Peyton, age 23.  I presume that J. H. Peyton is Catherine’s brother, James H. Pedan, who was born around 1833 or so.

In the 1870 Census, William and Hattie are still in Guernsey County.  The three boys are listed with them., but Lottie hasn’t come along yet.  She was born in 1875.  William died in 1878, leaving Hattie to raise the four children on her own.

By 1900, Hattie is living in Franklin County with her daughter.  Somewhere after 1900, Hattie and Lottie moved to California.  Hattie died there in 1911.  Her Death Certificate says her remains were shipped to Cambridge, Ohio, but it did not name which cemetery. Her Find A Grave # is 95574426.
