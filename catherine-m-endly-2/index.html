Children of Jacob Endly & Mary Rabe

Catherine M. Endly

Daughter of Jacob Endly and Mary Rabe

Next child of Jacob and Mary Rabe Endly  was Catherine.  She was born 03 May 1823 in Old Washington, Guernsey  County, Ohio.  Catherine married Albert G. Lawrence 18 Oct 1843 in Guernsey County and they had seven children: Alice M. Lawrence (1845-1860), Mary Rachel Lawrence (1847-1848), Albert Endly Lawrence (1848-1922), Lenora Lawrence (1851-1860), Catherine L. Lawrence (1855-1939), Ada V. Lawrence (1858-1943),  and Charles C. Lawrence (1864-1926).

Catherine M. Endly Lawrence died 21 May 1900 in Wheeling, West Virginia. She is buried in Old  Washington Cemetery, Guernsey County, Ohio.  Her Find A Grave # is 35575731.
