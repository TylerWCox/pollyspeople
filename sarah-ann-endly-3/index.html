Sarah Ann Endly

Daughter of Jacob Endly and Mary Rabe

I thought I’d done a page on Sarah Ann, but I must not have saved it.  There isn’t much to tell, actually.  She was born ca 1821 in Old Washington, Guernsey  County, Ohio.   Sarah married Edward Mc Kelvey 26 May 1841 in Guernsey County and they had two known children:  Charles M. McKelvey and Sophia McKelvey. In 1870 Charles and  Sophia are in the household of their aunt, Catherine Endly Lawrence.  I don’t know if the parents were dead by then, but both Charles and Sophia were old enough to be on their own so ?????
